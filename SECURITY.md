## Reporting a Vulnerability

We take the security of our tools and the systems they protect very seriously. If you identify a security issue, vulnerability, or binary integrity flaw:

1. **Do not create a public issue**.
2. Email full technical details and proof-of-concept information directly to:
   **`jayd424000@gmail.com`**
3. Please include:
   - Operating system version and build number.
   - Specific component or command line flags used.
   - Steps to reproduce or observe the behavior.
4. We will acknowledge receipt within 48 business hours and provide a remediation timeline.
